Privacy Policy - Snake Master

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Welcome to Snake Master. This Privacy Policy explains how Snake Master collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile game.

Snake Master is a mobile game published by Shahab Udin (Shahab Millizai).

1. Information We Collect

Snake Master does not require users to create an account, register, or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other contact information to play the game.

Gameplay takes place locally on your device. The game does not operate its own user account database.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Snake Master uses the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Yandex and its advertising partners may process certain information to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent advertising fraud, and improve advertising services.

Depending on SDK configuration and device permissions, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • IP address.
  • Device identifiers and technical device information.
  • Operating system and device information.
  • Internet connection and network information.
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, and interactions.

Yandex may share or process this information with its advertising partners according to its own privacy policy and applicable laws.

Read the Yandex privacy policy: Yandex Advertising Privacy Policy .

3. Location Information

Snake Master does not request GPS permission or directly access the player's precise or approximate physical location for gameplay or location-based services.

The default configuration of the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK does not collect precise or approximate location. Location transmission is an optional feature that requires additional configuration, appropriate permissions, and user consent.

4. External Links

Snake Master may provide an external sponsor link. When users open an external website, that website may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Snake Master does not require children to create accounts or submit personal contact information.

Advertising services and age-related settings must be configured according to the intended audience and applicable child privacy laws.

6. Data Security

We do not maintain a personal account database for Snake Master.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their security practices, privacy policies, and applicable laws.

7. Advertising Choices

Users may be able to reset or limit their advertising identifier through their Android device settings, depending on device availability and applicable rules.

Users can also review the privacy and advertising choices provided by Yandex.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about personal data processed in connection with Snake Master.

Since Snake Master does not maintain its own user account database, advertising data requests may need to be directed to the relevant advertising provider.

To submit a privacy question or request to the developer, use the contact information below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, advertising services, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
